# Q1(e) Demo Video Script: ESG Sustainability Support Assistant

**Target length:** 1 min 45 s (must stay under 2:00)
**Audience:** senior executives
**Record with:** QuickTime (File → New Screen Recording, microphone on) or Microsoft Stream / Clipchamp
**Before recording:** open the Dialogflow CX console with the Default Start Flow canvas showing and the Test Agent panel closed. Zoom the browser to 110%.

---

| Time | Show on screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Flow canvas (Default Start Flow) | "Good morning. Today we waste water, energy and money because problems on our sites are reported late, by email, or not at all. This is the Sustainability Support Assistant, a Dialogflow CX chatbot that lets any employee report a sustainability issue in under a minute." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Hover across the canvas from Start Page to Collect Incident Details, Confirm Report, Submit and Route | "Every report follows the same path. The bot collects four facts: what the issue is, where it is, what's happening, and whether anyone is at risk. It confirms them with the employee, then routes the report to the team that owns the fix." |
| 0:30–1:05 | Open Test Agent and type the Test 1 messages: `Hi`, `There is a water leak in Building C`, `Level 1 bathroom near the server room`, `Water spreading across the floor towards a power board`, `yes` | "Here's a live example. The employee reports a water leak. Notice that the bot already understood the category from the first sentence, so it only asks for the location and details. When the employee says someone could be at risk, the bot puts safety first. It gives the emergency number and marks the report as critical." |
| 1:05–1:20 | Type `yes`, then `no` | "After confirmation, the report goes straight to Facilities and Energy, with a clear review time. Reports are anonymous unless the employee chooses to give an email." |
| 1:20–1:35 | Reset, then type `I want to report my manager` | "We also designed for responsible use. Conduct concerns are never recorded by the bot. They're directed to our confidential Speak Up channels, and a person is always one message away." |
| 1:35–1:50 | Back to the canvas | "The value is simple: faster fixes, less waste, and structured data that feeds our ESG reporting. The next step is connecting it to our ticketing system and sustainability dashboard. Thank you." |
